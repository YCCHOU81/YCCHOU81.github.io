I am an HCMC Fellow at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS), working in algebraic geometry with interests in enumerative geometry, quantum K-theory, and integrable systems. My recent research focuses on two main directions: quantum K-theory and Gopakumar--Vafa invariants, and the geometry of generalized Lamé equations and their monodromy. I received my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Utah in May 2022 under the supervision of Y.P. Lee, and previously held a postdoctoral position at Academia Sinica. Here is my CV.
