Cell counts and cone angles

On the quotient sphere the four marked vertices have degrees dᵢ = 2nᵢ + 1. The double cover branched at p₀,…,p₃ is a torus. It contains 2N hemispheres, has area 4πN, and has cone angle 2πdᵢ = (4nᵢ + 2)π above pᵢ. Weight zero is a smooth marked point upstairs.
